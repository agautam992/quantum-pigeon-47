How to rename many files at once

  1. Choose your files. Click the big box in step 1, or drag your files onto it. You can add more later.
  2. Pick how to rename them. In step 2, tap an option such as New name & numbers, Add words, Replace a word, Capital letters, Tidy up or File type. A green dot shows which ones are on.
  3. Check and download. In step 3 you see the old name crossed out and the new name next to it. When it looks right, press the download button.

Examples

  • Holiday_001, Holiday_002… Open New name & numbers, type Holiday as the name and switch on Add numbers.
  • Remove a word from every name. Open Replace a word, type the word in the first box and leave the second box empty.
  • Tidy messy downloads. Open Tidy up and change spaces to underscores, then open Capital letters and pick all small letters.
  • Add the year to the start. Open Add words and type 2026_ in the first box.

If something is shown in red

Two files may end up with the same name, or a name may contain a symbol that is not allowed (\ / : * ? " < > |). Use the Fix automatically buttons that appear, or change your options.

Why do I get copies?

A web page cannot change files on your computer directly, so you download renamed copies. Your original files are never touched. Several files arrive together in one ZIP, which you can open to get them out.
