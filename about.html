About Soul Memoir

Soul Memoir is a free online tool for renaming many files at once. It began as an Android app and now works in any modern browser on phones, tablets and computers.

It is built to be fast, simple and private. All the work happens on your device, and your files are never uploaded to a server.

Questions or ideas? Visit the contact page.
